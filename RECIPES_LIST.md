## Image Generation Tips

### Style Guidelines:
- **Photography style**: Professional food photography, natural lighting
- **Composition**: Close-up shots showing texture and details
- **Background**: Rustic Turkish elements (copper plates, traditional tiles, wooden surfaces)
- **Colors**: Warm tones, emphasize Turkish red (#C41E3A) where appropriate
- **Garnish**: Fresh herbs (parsley, mint), lemon slices, pomegranate seeds

### AI Prompt Template:
```
Professional food photography of [dish name], Turkish cuisine,
rustic presentation on traditional copper/ceramic plate,
natural lighting, warm color palette with Turkish red accents,
garnished with fresh herbs, shallow depth of field,
high resolution, appetizing composition
```

### Example Prompts:

**For Adana Kebab:**
```
Professional food photography of Adana kebab, spicy minced lamb kebab
on metal skewers, served with grilled vegetables and lavash bread,
traditional Turkish copper tray, natural lighting,
garnished with parsley and lemon wedges, warm tones
```

**For Baklava:**
```
Professional food photography of Turkish baklava, golden phyllo layers
with crushed pistachios, drizzled with syrup, on ornate Turkish plate,
garnished with whole pistachios, shallow depth of field,
warm lighting highlighting the layers
```

**For Turkish Coffee (if adding):**
```
Professional food photography of Turkish coffee in traditional copper
cezve and small ornate cup, foam on top, served with Turkish delight,
on decorative Ottoman-style tray, warm atmospheric lighting
```
